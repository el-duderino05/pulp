# Lists

A tight bullet list:

- one
- two
- three

A numbered list starting at 3:

3. three
4. four

A list starting at 0:

0. zero
1. one

Four adjacent lists of each kind:

- first

* second

- third

* fourth

1. first

1) second

1. third

1) fourth

A loose list, because an item has two paragraphs:

- First paragraph.

  Second paragraph.

  - nested bullet

- Next item

A loose list, because a nested numbered list starts at 2:

- a

  2. b

- c

A tight list with nested lists, indented by the marker width:

1. one
   - bullet\
     continued
     1. deep
2. two

Wide numbers:

9. nine

10. ten

    More about ten.

11. eleven

    - nested under eleven

Item text that needs escapes:

- \- dash
- 3\. three
- &#32;space
- &nbsp;
